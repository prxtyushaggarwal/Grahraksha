Problem Statement

SIH26001

Landslide Risk Early-Warning System for India's Mountain Corridors

India's mountainous regions face significant landslide threats during monsoons, affecting millions of lives and critical infrastructure. GrahRaksha addresses this challenge by combining real-time weather data, geological surveys, satellite imagery, and machine learning to provide actionable early warnings to at-risk communities.
